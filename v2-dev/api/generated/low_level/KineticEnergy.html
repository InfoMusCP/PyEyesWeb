KineticEnergy#

Weighted kinetic energy of a set of labelled 3D points, such as the joints of a skeleton.

Layer 2: low-level features timescale: up to ~0.5 s EyesWeb: KineticEnergyIndex (KineticEnergy)

Alternative constructors#

KineticEnergy.from_position(skeleton=None, *, weights=None, reference_point='', sampling_rate=None)[source]#

Kinetic energy of joint positions (the EyesWeb mode).

Each frame holds the positions (N, 3) of the joints, or a dict label -> (x, y, z). Stateful: the speed is the difference to the previous frame over the time step, so the first frame gives 0. Takes the parameters of KineticEnergy except input_mode.

KineticEnergy.from_velocity(skeleton=None, *, weights=None, reference_point='', sampling_rate=None)[source]#

Kinetic energy of joint velocities (an extension, not in EyesWeb).

Each frame holds the velocities (N, 3) of the joints, or a dict label -> (vx, vy, vz). Stateless: 0.5 * w * |v|^2 per frame from the first one on, with no time step and no reference_point. Takes the parameters of KineticEnergy except input_mode.

Description#

class pyeyesweb.low_level.KineticEnergy(labels=None, skeleton=None, *, input_mode='position', weights=None, reference_point='', sampling_rate=None)[source]#

Weighted kinetic energy of a set of labelled 3D points, such as the joints of a skeleton.

The kinetic energy of the body is the amount of motion it carries, with fast and heavy segments counting more. In "position" mode (default) the speed \(\mathbf{v}_i\) of joint \(i\) is the displacement between two consecutive frames divided by the time step; in "velocity" mode the frames already hold the velocities. With \(w_i\) the weight of the joint (an approximation of its mass),

\[E = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i} w_i \, \lVert \mathbf{v}_i \rVert^2 .\]

Only the joints listed in weights contribute (a joint missing from an explicit weights mapping weighs 0). If reference_point names a joint, the positions are taken relative to it (position mode), so that the translation of the whole body does not count. The time step of a call is the difference to the previous timestamp if given, else 1 / sampling_rate, else one second per frame.

Parameters:
labelssequence of str, optional

Joint names, in the order of the rows of an array frame. Needed for array input; a dict frame carries its own names. Taken from skeleton when that is given.

skeletonstr or Skeleton, optional

A skeleton preset ("kinect_v2", …) or definition. It sets labels and weights (the preset weights, or 1.0 for every joint where a preset defines none).

input_mode{“position”, “velocity”}, optional

"position" (default): the frames hold positions and the feature differentiates them; the first frame gives 0. "velocity": the frames hold velocities and the feature applies the formula directly (no time step, no reference_point). This mode is an extension: EyesWeb takes positions only. KineticEnergy.from_position(...) and KineticEnergy.from_velocity(...) build one or the other. (structural)

weightsmapping of str to float or sequence of float, optional

Weight \(w_i\) of each joint (dimensionless, or a mass in the unit of your choice; the energy is in that unit times the squared speed). A mapping joint -> weight or a sequence with one weight per label (a different length raises ValueError). Defaults to 1.0 for every joint of labels, or to the preset weights of skeleton; a joint missing from an explicit mapping weighs 0. Without labels, skeleton or weights the constructor raises ValueError. (live)

reference_pointstr, optional

Joint the positions are taken relative to, in position mode. Defaults to "" (none). (live)

sampling_ratefloat, optional

Sampling rate of the frames in Hz, used when no timestamp is given. Defaults to None (one second per frame, and the reason says so). (live)

Notes

Inputs. One skeleton frame per call: an (N, 3) array in the order of labels or a dict label -> (x, y, z). process(frame, *, timestamp=None); run(frames, *, timestamps=None) takes a (T, N, 3) recording. timestamp is in seconds.

Outputs. kinetic_energy: non-negative scalar, in weights times (length/s)^2 (length per frame in position mode without a time).

Statuses. OK from the first frame (the first frame of position mode gives 0, there is no previous frame); INVALID_INPUT when the frame holds NaN or infinity, is empty, is a dict that lacks a weighted joint ("missing joints: ..."), or the timestamp does not increase. A rejected frame is not taken as the previous one: the next step is measured from the last valid frame.

Limitations: the weights are a weighting of the joints, not a body model; the presets without anthropometric weights (qualisys_sport, mediapipe, openpose) weight every joint by 1.0.

Examples

>>> import numpy as np
>>> from pyeyesweb.low_level import KineticEnergy
>>> frames = np.zeros((3, 2, 3))
>>> frames[:, 0, 0] = [0.0, 0.1, 0.2]  # joint 0 moves at 1 m/s
>>> frames[:, 1, 1] = [0.0, 0.2, 0.4]  # joint 1 moves at 2 m/s
>>> feature = KineticEnergy(labels=["hand", "elbow"], weights={"hand": 1.0, "elbow": 2.0}, sampling_rate=10.0)
>>> feature.run(frames).kinetic_energy.round(3)
array([0. , 4.5, 4.5])
