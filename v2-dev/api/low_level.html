    • Kinematics

    • Velocity, acceleration, jerk, curvature or direction of a moving point, from a Savitzky-Golay fit.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Kinematics and energy </theory/kinematics_energy>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/Kinematics>

    • KineticEnergy

    • Weighted kinetic energy of a set of labelled 3D points, such as the joints of a skeleton.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Kinematics and energy </theory/kinematics_energy>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/KineticEnergy>

    • BoundingBoxFilledArea

    • Contraction index of a 3D posture: convex hull area over bounding box area.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Shape, symmetry and balance </theory/shape_symmetry_balance>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/BoundingBoxFilledArea>

    • EllipsoidSphericity

    • Sphericity of the ellipsoid fitted to a point cloud, or of one given by its three axes.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Shape, symmetry and balance </theory/shape_symmetry_balance>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/EllipsoidSphericity>

    • PointsDensity

    • Dispersion of a set of 3D points around their centroid.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Shape, symmetry and balance </theory/shape_symmetry_balance>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/PointsDensity>

    • Symmetry

    • Left/right symmetry of a skeleton frame, as a ratio index or a mirror-reflection score.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Shape, symmetry and balance </theory/shape_symmetry_balance>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/Symmetry>

    • TriangleSymmetry

    • Symmetry of a head-and-shoulders triangle about its apex, from 0 to 1.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Shape, symmetry and balance </theory/shape_symmetry_balance>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/TriangleSymmetry>

    • SpectralArcLength

    • Spectral arc length (SPARC) of a speed profile: smoothness from the shape of its amplitude spectrum.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Smoothness </theory/smoothness>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/SpectralArcLength>

    • LogDimensionlessJerk

    • Log dimensionless jerk of a speed or acceleration profile: smoothness from the jerk of the movement.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Smoothness </theory/smoothness>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/LogDimensionlessJerk>

    • Harmonicity

    • Harmonicity of a speed profile: 1 for a single bell-shaped speed peak, lower for an irregular speed.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Smoothness </theory/smoothness>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/Harmonicity>

    • SubmovementCount

    • Number of sub-movements of a speed profile: its prominent speed peaks, at least 100 ms apart.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Smoothness </theory/smoothness>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/SubmovementCount>

    • CurvatureDispersion

    • Curvature-based smoothness index of a movement, from its velocity and acceleration vectors.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Smoothness </theory/smoothness>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/CurvatureDispersion>

    • CurvatureVelocityCorrelation

    • Correlation between curvature and velocity of a 1 to 3 channel trajectory, a smoothness index.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Smoothness </theory/smoothness>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/CurvatureVelocityCorrelation>

    • Equilibrium

    • Equilibrium of a barycenter within the elliptical support region between two feet.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Shape, symmetry and balance </theory/shape_symmetry_balance>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/Equilibrium>

    • DirectionChange

    • Direction change of a trajectory in a window: sharp turns of the path and the area it encloses.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Kinematics and energy </theory/kinematics_energy>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/DirectionChange>

    • FilterContour

    • Circular Gaussian smoothing of a closed 2D contour.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Imaging </theory/imaging>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/FilterContour>

    • ContourCurvature

    • Signed curvature at every point of a closed 2D contour.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Imaging </theory/imaging>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/ContourCurvature>

    • ConvexHull

    • Convex hull polygon of a 2D contour.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Imaging </theory/imaging>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/ConvexHull>

    • BodyPartsFromCurvature

    • Contour points at the curvature extrema of a silhouette, tracked as labelled body parts.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Imaging </theory/imaging>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/BodyPartsFromCurvature>

    • MotionNoiseDetector

    • Noise test of the skeleton motion: whether the motion is random, with jumpy speeds.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Kinematics and energy </theory/kinematics_energy>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/MotionNoiseDetector>

    • Directness

    • Directness index of a path: straight-line distance over path length, 1 for a straight line.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Kinematics and energy </theory/kinematics_energy>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/Directness>

    • TrajectoryNonPlanarity

    • Non-planarity of a 3D trajectory: the mean distance of its points from local best-fit planes.

    • up to ~0.5 s

    • :doc:Kinematics and energy </theory/kinematics_energy>

    • :doc:API </api/generated/low_level/TrajectoryNonPlanarity>
