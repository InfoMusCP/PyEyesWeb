Analysis primitives#

The analysis primitives are the measures that belong to no layer of the conceptual model (Conceptual framework) because they know nothing of bodies, skeletons or geometry: they take any scalar, vector or event signal and describe it. How regular a signal is, how periodic, how rare its latest value is, how two signals move together. They can be applied to a layer-1 signal, to the output of a low-level feature or to a mid-level one, and they have no timescale of their own; the timescale is that of the signal and of the window you give. The group has 19 features; this page helps to choose among them. The groups are described in Layers and the catalog.

Which feature for which question#

How regular or complex is the signal? SampleEntropy measures the irregularity of a window; CoarseGraining produces the same signal at a slower scale; MultiScaleEntropy combines both into a curve over scales and a complexity index [4], the input of the dominance analysis of High-level features. Rarity tells how rare the newest value is within a memory of past values. Theory: Entropy, rarity and periodicity.

Is it periodic, and are the pulses regular? Periodicity finds the periods of each channel of a window by the Periodicity Transform, with their normalised power; BinaryPulsesRegularity tells whether the pulses of a window are equally spaced. For the peaks of a signal use ScalarPeakDetector, which flags the samples at which a peak has been found (a maximum, reported up to window samples late; threshold is the swing of the signal, not a level). Theory: Entropy, rarity and periodicity and Gesture segmentation.

Do two or more signals move together? The synchronization measures are the largest family and are chosen by the kind of signal.

  • Continuous signals: PhaseLockingValue for the phase relation of two signals in a band, CorrelationsMatrix and PartialRecurrenceSynchronization for the correlation of a group and the direct link of each pair, and NonlinearInterdependence when the relation is not linear.

  • Recurrence: RecurrencePlot for when a movement returns to a state it visited, CrossRecurrencePlot for when one movement visits states of another, and GeneralisedAutoCorrelation for the recurrence rate as a function of the lag.

  • Events: EventSynchronization, EventSynchronizationMECS and SpikeSynchronization for trains of events, and SequenceDetector for a given sequence of integer events in a stream.

  • Staged processes: TemporalSynchronization follows the progression of a synchronization process from a chain of synchronization values.

Theory: Synchronization. The synchronization of a group of musicians is the setting of [5].

A typical pipeline#

A primitive takes any series, such as the output of another feature, a joint coordinate or a respiration trace. Here a sine and white noise are measured by the sample entropy of the last window, for template length 2: the regular signal has the lower entropy.

>>> import numpy as np
>>> from pyeyesweb.analysis_primitives import SampleEntropy
>>> rng = np.random.default_rng(1)
>>> n = np.arange(400)
>>> regular = np.sin(2 * np.pi * n / 40)
>>> irregular = rng.standard_normal(400)
>>> entropy = SampleEntropy(window=200, embedding_dimension=2)
>>> a = entropy.run(regular).sample_entropy[-1, -1]               # template length 2, last window
>>> b = entropy.run(irregular).sample_entropy[-1, -1]
>>> round(float(a), 2), round(float(b), 2)
(0.39, 2.19)

What to know before choosing#

  • A window feature takes window samples and hop, and is WARMING_UP until the window is full (Results, statuses and recorders). Choose the window in seconds first and multiply by the sampling rate: some metrics have minimum lengths, which the constructors check.

  • Primitives work on arrays and know nothing of units: the unit of the output follows from the input and is stated on each API page.

  • Synchronization measures take several signals in one call; event measures take trains of events, not continuous signals: build them first, for instance with ScalarPeakDetector.

  • Time is in seconds, and a feature that needs a rate takes sampling_rate (Time, units and parameters).

Hands-on: see Analysis primitives: complexity, rarity, periodicity, Synchronization of continuous signals and Synchronization of events.
