SpectralArcLength
=================

Spectral arc length (SPARC) of a speed profile: smoothness from the shape of its amplitude spectrum.

:bdg-primary:`Layer 2: low-level features` :bdg-secondary:`timescale: up to ~0.5 s`

Layer: :doc:`Layer 2: low-level features </api/low_level>`. Family: :doc:`Smoothness </theory/smoothness>`.
