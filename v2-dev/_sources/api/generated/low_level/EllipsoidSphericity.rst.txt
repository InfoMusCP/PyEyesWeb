EllipsoidSphericity
===================

Sphericity of the ellipsoid fitted to a point cloud, or of one given by its three axes.

:bdg-primary:`Layer 2: low-level features` :bdg-secondary:`timescale: up to ~0.5 s` :bdg-info:`EyesWeb: EllipsoidSphericity (EllipsoidSphericity3D)`
