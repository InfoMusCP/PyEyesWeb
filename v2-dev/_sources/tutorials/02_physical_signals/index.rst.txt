Physical signals
----------------

Layer 1: signals produced from raw frames or from a model: hit cells, humanoid and mass-spring simulation and prediction, silhouettes, videograms and stream plumbing.

This tutorial covers the ones that take points: HitCellDetector tells in which cell of a grid each point is, a mass-spring humanoid turns a captured skeleton into a smooth, plausible one, PointsPredictor and the generic models look a moment ahead, and GenericModelEstimator fits a model to a movement.">

This tutorial reduces the silhouette of a person to a few quantities that follow the movement: where its centre and its parts are (``SilhouetteCentroids``, SilhouetteBodyParts), how its images evolve over time (``Videogram``), whether a series of pulses is regular (``BinaryPulsesRegularity``), and how to cut asynchronous streams into windows that cover the same time (``TimeWindowSynchronizer``). The images are synthetic: the example data hold only motion capture.">
