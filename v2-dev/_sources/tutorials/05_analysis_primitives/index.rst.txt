Analysis primitives
-------------------

Metrics that apply to any signal at any layer: entropy, rarity, periodicity, peaks, and the synchronization family with the high-level dominance.

It answers one question about any series of numbers. This tutorial uses six of them: how irregular a signal is at one and at several time scales (``SampleEntropy``, CoarseGraining, MultiScaleEntropy), who is the most regular of a group (``EnsembleDominance``), how unusual the newest value is (``Rarity``), when a peak happens (``ScalarPeakDetector``) and which period repeats (``Periodicity``).">
